Inside

Open, flowing space.

Ten-foot ceilings and durable tile run through the living room, dining area and kitchen. Each area feels distinct while staying visually connected, so nothing is closed off and light and views reach every end of the house. From the living room, the view opens onto the peaceful retreat of the backyard.

The kitchen has granite counters, stainless appliances, recessed lighting and a breakfast bar. Modern light fixtures on dimmers set the mood, bright for meal prep and low for a quiet evening.

Quiet, and easy to cool

The living room and dining room windows were replaced in 2025 with Anlin Panoramic low-E glass. They open easily, filter the sun and heat, and keep outside sound out. With mature trees shading the hottest part of the day, utility costs stay low. Add your 12-month average

Rest

Three bedrooms, no stairs.

The primary suite fits a king-size bed and has its own bath and walk-in closet, a custom walnut headboard and solid maple floating nightstands with dimmable lighting. The other two bedrooms share the second full bath.

Straight answers

Why are you selling?

I'm relocating out of state, and I have flexibility on the timeline.

How did you arrive at the price?

Recent nearby sales and the appraisal, if you get one

How do showings work?

The home is owner-occupied, so showings are by appointment. Buyers without an agent are asked for a pre-approval letter or proof of funds and a photo ID, which are deleted after the showing. For weekend showings, documents are due by noon on Friday.

Do you work with buyer's agents?

Yes. I'm open to all written offers. Feel free to include your requested commission in the contract. I evaluate every offer on its net terms.

How do I make an offer?

Use the standard TREC One to Four Family Residential Contract and email it with a pre-approval letter or proof of funds dated within the last 30 days. Title is through Rise Title. Please allow 24 to 48 hours for a response.
